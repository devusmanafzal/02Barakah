The origin

Why we built this

Every project here started as a real need. We wanted tools that understood the rhythm of a Muslim life — not as an afterthought, but by design. A better way to plan Qiyam al-Layl. A space for reflection. A community that builds together.

As builders by nature and believers by conviction, we kept returning to one idea: that technology built with sincere intention carries a different kind of weight. The Prophet ﷺ said actions are by their intentions — and we believe that extends to what we build.

Zero2Barakah is our attempt to put that belief into practice, one project at a time — starting from zero and building towards something with Barakah.
