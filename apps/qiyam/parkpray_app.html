How it works

A simple way to share parking around prayer time.

Park & Pray is a small, community-run pool of parking spots near the mosque. Neighbours share the use of their spot, and worshippers can hold one for the length of a prayer.

  1. 01

    Check availability

    Open this page on your way to the mosque and see instantly which shared spots are free.

  2. 02

    Reserve in one tap

    Pick a spot, enter your name and phone, and it's held in your name for up to 60 minutes.

  3. 03

    Park and pray

    Drive straight there without circling the block. Arrive calm, not late.

  4. 04

    It releases itself

    Your hold expires automatically after 60 minutes — or release it the moment you leave for the next brother or sister.

A note on trust

Please respect the owner's spot — keep it clean, don't block driveways, and release your reservation as soon as you leave. This service runs entirely on trust and good intentions.
